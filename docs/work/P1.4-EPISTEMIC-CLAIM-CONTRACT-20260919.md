## What landed

### 1. Minimal claim surface — `ledger/claims.py` (new)

A tiny typed claim envelope implementing the P1.4 "deliver only what canonical identity
needs immediately" scope (SCHEMA.md §2.2 is the broader direction; nothing generalized
was built):

- claim kinds: `declared` / `observed` / `inferred` (only `declared` is emitted today);
- `sidecar_declaration_claim(observation)` — a deterministic ``declared`` claim carrying
  `claim_id`, `field`, `value`, `subject_*`, `source_id`, `observed_at`;
- `divergent_declarations(observations, fields={project_key})` — returns the typed
  `declared` claims for observations that disagree on an identity field (empty when they
  agree).

Claims are descriptive evidence, never merge authority — they never change which
observations belong to which project.

### 2. `DIVERGENT_SIDECAR_DECLARATIONS` review — `ledger/identity.py`

After canonical projects are compiled, any project with >=2 member observations whose
sidecar declarations diverge (and no operator `canonical_key` already chosen) opens a
bounded review:

- references both conflicting observation IDs;
- carries the differing typed `declared` claims as evidence;
- leaves the remote-based merge intact (advisory — `reason_automation_stopped` makes
  clear no value was chosen by last-write-wins);
- resolves via a `canonical_key` decision (operator-chosen authoritative key) — the
  P2.8-style ratchet: an explicit key supercedes the divergence, and the review does not
  recur on unchanged inputs.

Observations that agree, or that declare nothing conflicting, open no review.
